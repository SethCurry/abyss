This is the initial release of Abyss with its initial feature set, including:

- Websocket proxies of ACP connections
- Intercepting ACP read/write/terminal commands
- Creating bind mounts
- Copying files into the container
- Executing setup scripts

You can get a copy of the release at from the [releases page](https://github.com/SethCurry/abyss/releases).
